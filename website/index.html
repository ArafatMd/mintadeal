How it works

Your neighbourhood, in one app

Four ways to get it

However the shop works, it works

Every shop chooses what suits it. The app shows you only what works for your address.

🛵

Shop delivery

The shop's own staff deliver within their area. The fee, if any, is shown before you order.

🚚

Mintadeal delivery

For shops without riders, a Mintadeal partner picks up and delivers. You hand over a code at the door.

🏪

Pickup

Order ahead and collect from the counter when the shop marks it ready. No waiting, no queue.

🎟️

Venue offers

Salon, restaurant, hotel and event offers. Buy in the app and show your QR code at the venue.

Questions

Good to know

Is Mintadeal only in Bengaluru?

Yes, for now. We are starting with neighbourhoods in Bengaluru, beginning around HSR Layout, Koramangala and nearby areas, and adding more localities as shops join.

Do I need a coupon code?

No. Prices in the app already include the offer. Add to cart and order; the discount is applied automatically.

How do I pay?

Pay online with UPI, cards or wallets through Razorpay, or choose to pay the shop on delivery or pickup where the shop allows it. Venue offers are paid online.

What if the shop cannot take my order?

Shops accept or decline within 15 minutes. If a shop declines or does not respond, the order is cancelled and any online payment is refunded in full.

How do refunds work?

Refunds go back to the way you paid, usually within 5 to 7 working days. Read the refund and cancellation policy for details.

I own a shop. How do I join?

Open the shop app, sign in with your mobile number and fill in your shop details. We review every shop before it goes live. Read more for shops.
