Who it is for

Any local business with something to offer

Grocery and provision stores, meat and fish shops, bakeries, clothing stores and showrooms, electronics and hardware shops, restaurants, hotels, salons, gyms, services and events.

📝

Sign up in minutes

Open the shop app, sign in with your mobile number and add your shop: address, opening hours, logo and a cover photo.

✅

We review your shop

Mintadeal checks every shop before it goes live, so customers can trust what they see.

🏷️

Post products and offers

Add photos, prices and offer prices. Products can be delivered or picked up; venue offers come with a QR code.

You stay in control

Deliver your way

Choose any combination. Customers only see the options that work for their address.

🛵

Your own delivery

Set how far you deliver, your delivery fee and a minimum order.

🚚

Mintadeal partner

No rider free? Book a Mintadeal delivery partner for an order. The trip cost is shown before you book.

🏪

Pickup

Customers order ahead and collect from your counter when you mark the order ready.

📷

Venue QR

Sell offers used at your venue. Scan the customer's QR code with the shop app; each code works once.

Money, clearly

You see every rupee

Your earnings screen shows each order, Mintadeal's commission and any delivery partner charges, and what will be paid to you next.

  • A commission only on completed orders, shown on every order
  • Online payments settled to your bank account or UPI
  • Pay-at-shop orders: the customer pays you directly
  • Partner delivery trips charged only when you book one

Current rates and any onboarding or listing charges are shared before you go live. Ask us.
