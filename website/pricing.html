Pricing

Simple, upfront pricing

Free for customers. Shops pay only when they make a sale.

🛍️

Customers

Free

No sign-up fee, no membership, no convenience or platform fee. You pay the shop's price, plus the shop's delivery fee if you choose delivery.

🏪

Shops

5% per completed order

No joining fee and no monthly fee. Commission is on the value of the items sold, only when an order is completed.

🚚

Partner delivery

₹30 + ₹8 per km

Only when a shop books a Mintadeal delivery partner for an order. The cost is shown before booking.

⛺

Pop-up stalls

Free during launch

Street, market and event stalls list for free for now. Any listing fee is shown before you start a stall.

Last updated 12 November 2026. All prices in Indian rupees (₹).

For customers

  • Using Mintadeal, browsing deals and following shops is free.
  • The price you pay is the price set by the shop, shown on every product and offer, including any discount.
  • Delivery fees and minimum order amounts are set by each shop and shown in your cart before you pay. Pickup and venue offers have no delivery fee.
  • Mintadeal adds no convenience fee, platform fee or payment charge. Pay online with UPI, debit or credit card or net banking through Razorpay, or pay the shop on delivery or pickup where the shop allows it.
  • Pop-up stalls are paid directly at the stall; Mintadeal takes no payment for them.

Your earnings screen shows every order, the commission on it and any delivery partner charges. Rates for some categories or shops may be agreed separately; any agreed rate is shown in your shop app.

Example

A customer orders items worth ₹1,000 and pays your delivery fee of ₹30 online, ₹1,030 in all. When the order is completed:

  • Commission: 5% of ₹1,000 = ₹50
  • Your payout for this order: ₹1,030 − ₹50 = ₹980
  • If you had booked a delivery partner for a 3 km trip instead of delivering yourself: ₹30 + 3 × ₹8 = ₹54, so your payout would be ₹926.

For orders paid to you on delivery or pickup, you keep the money, and the commission is taken from your next payout.

For pop-up stalls

Opening a stall is free while Mintadeal launches in Bengaluru. If a listing fee is introduced it will be a small amount per day, shown on screen before you start the stall, and paid online (or from your shop balance for shops). Fees are not refunded when a stall is stopped early or taken down for breaking our terms.

Taxes, refunds and changes

  • Shops' prices include any taxes the shop must charge. Where GST applies to Mintadeal's charges to shops, it is added on top.
  • Refunds for customers follow our refund and cancellation policy.
  • We tell shops at least 15 days before any change to these charges. Questions? Contact us.
