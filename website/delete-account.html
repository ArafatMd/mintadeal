Your account

Delete your account

For the Mintadeal app, Mintadeal for Shops and Mintadeal Delivery.

Last updated 3 November 2026

In the app

  1. Open the app and sign in with your phone number.
  2. Customers: go to Account. Shops: go to My shop. Pop-up stalls: open your stall screen and scroll down. Riders: go to Profile.
  3. Tap Delete my account and confirm.

Customer, rider and pop-up stall accounts are deleted straight away. A shop may have orders, payouts or charges to settle, so for shop owners the app sends the request to us and we close the shop and delete the account within 7 days.

Without the app

Write to us from the Contact page with the phone number of the account. We will confirm it is your number and delete the account within 7 days.

What is deleted

  • Your name, email address and profile photo.
  • Your phone number is removed from the account, so you can sign up again later as a new account.
  • Any live pop-up stall is ended and no longer shown to customers.

What we keep, and for how long

Records of orders, payments, refunds and payouts are kept for 8 years because Indian tax and accounting law requires it. They are not linked to your name, photo or phone number on your account any more, and are only used for accounts, tax and disputes. Reviews you wrote stay on the shop's page without your name.
